### 3. The problem

**Heading:** Proof gets rebuilt at every step. It shouldn't.

- **Skills and checks are proved again at every job.** Employers can't trust what
  a worker says they can do, so skills are retested and DBS, right to work and
  qualifications checked again, even when they're still valid.
- **Evidence is pieced together before every audit.** Enrolment, off-the-job
  hours, reviews and assessment sit in separate tools, so provider teams rebuild
  the evidence by hand.
- **Apprentices finish with nothing they can carry.** What an apprentice achieved
  lives in the provider's system. Their next employer can't see it or trust it
  without starting again.

### 4. Continuo

**For employers and agencies in regulated work**

**Heading:** Continuo. Verified skills for the workforce.

**Body:** Continuo proves what a worker can do, backed by the checks that show
they're cleared to do it, and keeps that proof true between jobs, not just at the
moment of hire. The record belongs to the worker, and it's free for them, always.

#### The core of Continuo: verified skills, not claimed ones

Every skill on a Continuo record is confirmed by the trainer, assessor or employer
who saw it, with the date and the evidence. Employers hire and deploy on what
workers can actually do, not on what a CV says.

- Each skill confirmed by whoever assessed or observed it, with the date and
  evidence attached
- Described against recognised occupational standards, so any employer can read them
- New skills confirmed in every role, so the record grows with the worker's career
- Shared by the worker with one link, so the next employer deploys them from day one

#### Verified checks

- Identity, right to work, DBS, qualifications and site cards, captured at source
- Nothing added without the worker's consent
- Every employer adds conduct, attendance and performance confirmations alongside skills

#### A compliance evidence engine

- Proof organised by each regulated obligation, not buried in folders
- Gaps and upcoming lapses flagged before they become breaches
- Evidence packs for any regulator, funder or client audit, on demand

#### Continuous, not one-off

- Quarterly skills and competence sign-off, done by a manager in minutes on a phone
- A Health Score showing how fresh and complete each record is
- One view across bank, agency and multiple employers

#### Four ways to work with Continuo

- **Self-serve platform.** Employers verify skills, run checks and confirm records
  themselves.
- **Verify Bureau.** A managed service for large employers and agencies. We verify
  skills and checks and return a verified record.
- **Trust Score.** A worker's verified skills and compliance status, shared by the
  worker with hirers and platforms.
- **Framework Licence.** White-label skills and credential infrastructure for
  sector bodies to run their own schemes.

### 7. How it works

**Heading:** How it works

**Body:** Two products, two flows. Each works on its own.

**How Continuo works**

1. **Verify skills and checks.** Skills are confirmed by the trainer, assessor or
   employer who saw them. Identity, right to work and DBS are checked at source,
   with the worker's consent.
2. **Share.** The worker sends one link to an employer or agency, full or partial,
   time-limited or revocable.
3. **Onboard.** The employer deploys the worker on verified skills instead of
   retesting and repeating checks.
4. **Stay current.** New skills, renewals, live checks and quarterly sign-offs keep
   the record true at every job after.

**How AMS works**

1. **Enrol.** The provider enrols the apprentice and the employer in AMS, with
   funding and ILR in one place.
2. **Deliver.** Off-the-job hours, reviews and KSB evidence are logged as they happen.
3. **Reach gateway.** A Gateway Readiness Score shows who is ready for end-point
   assessment.
4. **Complete with a Passport.** The apprentice leaves with a verified Passport of
   what they achieved.

**Better together**

When an apprentice finishes, they can carry their Passport's verified achievements
into Continuo, with their consent. Their first employer onboards from proof on day one.
